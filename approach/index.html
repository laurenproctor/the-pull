How we work

From your business goal
to a shared launch.

We turn your growth goal into a partner shortlist, a shared idea, and a launch plan. One team coordinates both brands, keeps decisions moving, and reports what changed—so you can decide what to invest in next.

We stay connected to the work from strategy through delivery. Both brands know what’s being decided, who owns the next step, and what success is meant to look like.

01

Start with the ambition.

We listen to the business goal, the audience, the brand, and the constraints. Together, we define what would make this collaboration worthwhile.

02

Find the right partner.

We use audience analytics and effectiveness data from past collaborations across industries to identify promising partners. We look at who each brand reaches, where their audiences overlap or complement one another, and which partnerships have delivered meaningful results. Those insights help us assess the fit and shape a shortlist around your goals.

03

Create the chemistry.

Every partner needs a reason to participate. We connect audience insight, brand fit, and a distinctive idea to a proposition both teams can support.

04

Give the work a structure.

Agree the scope, responsibilities, costs, and decision points. Then bring the right creative, legal, production, and channel specialists around the project.

05

Deliver.

Bring the collaboration to its audience. We coordinate the launch and run the agreed marketing across paid, earned, and owned media: Google Ads, Meta, ChatGPT placements where available, newsletter sponsorships, press outreach, social posts, and the brands’ own channels. Every channel has a role, a budget, and a plan.

06

Learn.

Measure what the collaboration achieved. We bring together channel analytics, audience response, press coverage, and available sales or conversion data, then compare the results with the goals and baselines agreed at the start. You get a clear view of what worked, what remains uncertain, and what to change.

07

Encore.

Give a strong partnership somewhere to go next. That might mean the same partner with a bigger idea, a new market, a second edition, or a recurring collaboration. We use the results to recommend the next move—and whether another act is worth the investment.

A few things worth knowing

Before we meet.

Do you handle the actual collaboration?

Yes. We can manage the collaboration from partner discovery through launch and reporting, or support the stages your team needs. You can bring us in for intelligence research, best-practice guidance, recommendations, execution, or coordination of legal assistance through independent counsel. We agree the support you need before work begins.

Can we bring you an existing idea or partner?

Absolutely. We can help develop the proposition, bring the teams into alignment, and build the delivery and measurement plan around an existing relationship.

How do you price an engagement?

Our pricing page sets out the $5,000 Opportunity Sprint, managed collaborations from $30,000, and custom ongoing programs. Scope, external costs, milestones, and terms are agreed before paid work begins.

What are the Observatory and Partner Studio?

The Observatory is our curated collaboration research. Partner Studio is an interactive preview of our proposed workflow. Studio examples are illustrative; the public preview is not a secure client portal. Research additions save in your browser, and automated monitoring and shared accounts are not connected.

Do you work alongside our existing agency?

Yes. We have experience working with numerous agencies and internal teams. We fit into your existing processes, agree clear responsibilities, and bring the collaboration support your team needs.
