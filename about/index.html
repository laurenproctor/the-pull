Meet the founder · New York

Lauren
Proctor

Marketing strategist.
Entrepreneur. Builder.

Founder, The Pull

Lauren’s work connects how people discover brands, what makes them care, and what turns that interest into growth.

Her experience spans luxury-brand research at Scott Galloway’s L2, co-founding a marketing platform acquired by Twitter, and leading digital strategy at NielsenIQ. She has also led marketing at Jobbatical and built Storyworlding, a consultancy working with fashion, luxury, and culture-based brands.

Alongside strategy, Lauren builds digital products and the systems that support marketing: websites, content workflows, audience research, and measurement. That practical experience shapes The Pull’s promise to stay involved from the first partner conversation through creative development, launch, and results.
