Accessibility

Last updated: September 24, 2026 · Current product preview

Privacy noticeTerms of useCookie policyAccessibilityEditorial & rights policy

We want people to research, evaluate, and shape partnerships regardless of how they navigate the web.

Our target and current status

We aim to work toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA. This is a development target, not a certification. The full application has not completed an independent accessibility audit, and we do not claim full conformance.

Using the site

  • Use the Skip to content link to move past public navigation or the dashboard header.
  • Switch between light and dark mode from the page header. The default follows your system preference until you choose a mode.
  • The desktop sidebar can collapse to icons. Controls keep accessible names and hover labels; expand it to restore visible text.
  • Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between controls, Enter to follow links, and Space to operate buttons and checkboxes.
  • Cookie preferences use a dialog that can be closed with Escape. Your focus returns to the control that opened it.
  • Use browser zoom and text enlargement. New public and support pages adapt to narrower screens.
  • Reduced-motion preferences disable decorative animation and smooth scrolling in the shared page styles.
  • The Observatory calendar offers a dated-event list. Comment charts include daily-count tables when comment data exists.

Known limitations

The existing Partner Studio preview contains dense tables, small secondary labels, and some interactions that need broader keyboard and assistive-technology evaluation. Charts and older prototype screens may not yet offer equivalent experiences in every mode. Third-party social embeds, campaign images, and the optional feedback widget are outside our direct control; captions, descriptions, and navigation support vary.

You can reject optional media and open the original source instead. If a visual, document, chart, or interaction prevents you from accessing information, ask for a text explanation or an alternative format.

Tell us about a barrier

Email support@the---pull.com or use the support page. Include the page URL, what you were trying to do, what happened, and your preferred way to receive help. If useful, include your browser and assistive technology; sharing a disability or diagnosis is not required.

We will use reports to prioritize improvements. We do not currently publish a guaranteed response time. Please mark a request as an access barrier if it prevents you from completing an essential task.

How we review

Development checks cover page structure, labels, keyboard operation, focus visibility, responsive layout, cookie choices, and text alternatives where available. Further testing with assistive technologies and people with disabilities is still needed. This statement will be revised as the product changes.
