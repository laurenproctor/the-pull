A collaboration works better when everyone knows what they are agreeing to. The same goes for your browser.

Cookies and similar storage

A cookie is a small file a service stores in your browser. This preview also uses local storage, which stays on this device and is not automatically sent with every request. Our preference controls cover optional services that may use either technology.

An invitation, not an obligation

Accept optional enables feedback and social players. Reject optional keeps both off. Choose ingredients lets you enable one category independently. Reading the site and following original source links remain available either way. We do not use preselected optional checkboxes.

Changing your mind

Use Cookie settings in any footer, the dashboard sidebar, or the support page. Rejecting previously accepted feedback reloads the page to stop the loaded tool. Disabling social media removes player connections from the page. These controls do not erase reports already submitted or clear cookies owned by external domains; use your browser settings and the provider’s controls for those.

If storage is blocked, choices apply to the current page only and we explain that they could not be saved. Clearing site data removes your choice, so the notice will appear again. It can also erase your locally saved research.

What these controls do not change

Hosting requests are necessary to deliver the site. Images hosted by brands or publications may make requests to those hosts. Following an external link is your choice and is governed by the destination’s policies. No optional advertising or audience analytics integration is configured in the current application code.

For more information or a privacy request, read our privacy notice or email privacy@the---pull.com.
