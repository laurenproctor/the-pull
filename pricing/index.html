Before we get started

Clear expectations.
Better working relationships.

Is there a charge for the first conversation?

No. We start with a free 30-minute conversation about your goal, budget, timing, and whether The Pull is the right fit. It is an introduction, not a research or legal consultation. Paid work begins only after you agree the scope and fee.

Is the price charged to each brand?

The price covers the defined engagement. One brand can commission the work, or the participating brands can agree how to share the fee. We document who pays what; a $30,000 project does not automatically become $30,000 per brand.

Can we bring an existing partner or idea?

Yes. We scope around what is already in place and the work you need. An existing partner may reduce discovery work, while delivery or production complexity can increase the overall scope. We quote the engagement before starting.

What if a potential partner says no?

Research identifies fit; outreach establishes interest. We agree the prospects and outreach period in advance—for example, up to eight approved prospects over six weeks—then review progress together. If there is no viable partner, you can pause, revise the brief, or agree further work. There is no automatic open-ended outreach fee.

What changes the price?

Additional partners, markets, launches, creative rounds, original production requirements, longer marketing campaigns, or more complex coordination can change the fee. Any change in scope and cost is agreed in writing before the additional work begins.

How do payments work?

Payment schedules are included in your proposal. Managed projects use milestone billing; external commitments are approved and funded before they are placed. The agreement explains the work covered by each payment and what happens if a project is paused or canceled.

Do you guarantee sales, press coverage, or a signed partnership?

No. We agree the work, decisions, and measures we are responsible for, then report against them. Another brand’s participation, editorial coverage, and commercial results depend on factors beyond our control.

Do we need to buy a software subscription?

No. The public Observatory is free, and standard reporting is included in the agreed service. Partner Studio is currently a public workflow preview, not a secure client portal. Your proposal specifies how live project information will be shared.
