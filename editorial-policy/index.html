Evidence first, perspective clearly labeled

The Observatory brings public collaboration information together with editorial analysis. Source links, observation dates, and qualifications should make it possible to distinguish a documented fact from a reported claim, estimate, or opinion. A source’s existence does not make every claim in it independently verified.

What the numbers mean

Coverage counts describe saved records, not a census of the internet. A missing record does not establish that no post or collaboration exists. Views, likes, comments, reach, sales, and incremental impact are different measures and must not be treated as interchangeable.

Comment sentiment describes only the collected, classified third-party sample. Brand-authored and unclassified comments are excluded from sentiment percentages. Top comment means the highest visible like count among saved comments with a known like count, not necessarily the top comment on the original platform.

Editorial independence and brand relationships

Inclusion in research does not mean a brand is a customer, partner, or endorser of The Pull. The site also contains explicitly illustrative Partner Studio examples. Any future sponsored study, paid placement, or material relationship relevant to an assessment should be disclosed alongside that content.

Ownership and reuse

Referenced names, trademarks, posts, photographs, and articles belong to their respective owners. Links and embeds are offered for research context. They do not grant you permission to reproduce assets in a campaign or commercial presentation. Obtain the relevant rights from the owner before reuse.

Corrections and removal requests

Email support@the---pull.com with the study URL, the material at issue, a description of the concern, supporting source links, and how we can contact you. If you act for a rights holder, explain your authority. Do not include unnecessary personal information.

For a privacy concern involving a person or comment, use privacy@the---pull.com. A report is a request for review; it does not itself determine ownership or the accuracy of a claim. We may correct, qualify, replace, or remove material as appropriate.
