Privacy notice

Last updated: September 24, 2026 · Current product preview

This notice describes the current public preview. The Pull is the product name; registered operator details must be finalized before a commercial service launches. Privacy contact: privacy@the---pull.com.

What this site stores

The current preview has no account registration, payment system, or shared research database. The role selector shows example experiences; it is not authentication. Do not enter passwords, confidential client material, sensitive personal information, or customer lists.

  • Browser-local records: profile details you save, research records, collected comments, and collection history stay in this browser’s local storage. This app does not upload those records to a shared application database.
  • Appearance preferences: your light/dark choice and sidebar layout are stored locally until changed or cleared.
  • Privacy preferences: we store your optional-service choices and the time of your choice locally for up to 180 days.
  • Support messages: if you send us email, we receive your email address, message, and attachments. The support-page composer opens your email application; it does not submit a ticket itself.
  • Optional feedback: if you enable BugHerd, it can process page URLs, browser and device details, and feedback or screenshots you submit. Avoid showing private information in screenshots.
  • Hosting and connections: Netlify serves the site. Like other hosts, it processes technical requests, which can include IP addresses, request paths, and browser information.

Research about public activity

Studies include public brand names, account handles, post links, editorial sources, and observed metrics. A collected comment may include its author’s public handle, text, publication date, likes, and a manually reviewed sentiment label. Source links identify where an observation came from. Report an inaccurate attribution or a rights concern using the contact below.

Why information is used

Information supports the features you request, remembers your choices, helps us investigate problems, and documents collaboration research. Where applicable, essential operation and security rely on legitimate interests in running the service; support correspondence is used to answer your request; optional embedded services rely on consent. This preview does not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.

You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact us to explain your request. You can withdraw consent for optional tools through Cookie settings at any time.

External services and media

BugHerd and embedded YouTube or Instagram players load only after the relevant optional category is accepted. Brand and editorial images may load directly from their source hosts, which receive normal connection information. Opening a source link takes you to that provider’s site and its own privacy practices.

  • Netlify privacy policy — hosting.
  • BugHerd privacy policy — optional feedback.
  • Google privacy policy — optional YouTube players.
  • Meta privacy policy — optional Instagram players.

These providers may process information internationally, including in the United States. Their policies explain their processing and transfer practices. No advertising pixels, audience analytics tags, or sale-of-data integrations are configured in this preview.

How long information remains

Local profile and research records remain until you remove them or clear this site’s browser data. Browser storage is not a backup and can be lost. Privacy preferences expire after 180 days. Support correspondence and feedback should be retained only as needed to handle the request, resolve related issues, and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations; provider records also follow their own retention practices.

Your choices and requests

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or obtain a copy of information, object to processing, withdraw consent, or appeal a request decision. Email privacy@the---pull.com with the relevant page or record and the action you are requesting. Do not send identity documents unless a proportionate verification step is requested.

We cannot remotely retrieve or erase data that exists only in your browser. Individual added social records have a Remove local record control. For all local data, use your browser’s site-data controls. Clearing site data also removes saved research and profile changes.

You may contact the relevant privacy regulator where you live, work, or believe a concern occurred, including the UK ICO where applicable. Rights requests should not lead to discriminatory treatment.

Children, security, and changes

This preview is intended for adult professional use and is not directed to children. If a child’s personal information was submitted, contact us. No website or storage method can promise absolute security. We will update this notice when the site’s data practices change and seek fresh consent where required.
