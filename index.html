Before you jump in

A few things worth knowing.

Do you handle the actual collaboration, or just recommend partners?

The service covers the collaboration itself. We can handle partner research and outreach, concept development, partner alignment, and coordination through launch and reporting. The exact deliverables, specialist support, and decision-making responsibilities are agreed before work begins.

Can we bring a partner or an idea we already have?

Yes. We can start with an existing relationship or concept and help turn it into a proposition, delivery plan, and measurement approach both brands can agree on.

What does “one-stop” include?

One coordinated engagement across strategy and execution. Depending on the project, that may include specialist legal, creative, production, PR, or other support. Professional advice, third-party costs, and each team’s obligations are defined in the engagement; browsing this website does not create those relationships.

Can I explore the software?

Yes. The Observatory contains curated research and the Partner Studio preview shows our proposed workflow. The preview is public and is not a secure client portal; do not enter confidential material. Automated monitoring and shared accounts are not connected.

How is an engagement priced?

We scope the work around your objective, the stage of the partnership, and what you need us to deliver. Fees, external costs, milestones, and terms are agreed before a paid engagement. Start with an email to support@the---pull.com.
