THE RITUAL · BRUT × FAUX

A hospitality ritual guests experience before they realize it is a collaboration.

A designed object enters service naturally, becomes part of the visit, and can later become something guests take home.

FX
FAUX
West Village · Hospitality
High evidenceOpportunity window open

Turn a West Village hospitality ritual into something guests experience, photograph, and ultimately own.

BRUT gains lived downtown context instead of another fashion placement. FAUX gains a proprietary guest ritual with an editorial and retail life beyond the venue.

What becomes possible together

BRUT can make a beautiful object. FAUX can create a memorable hospitality ritual. Together, the object becomes part of a repeated real-world behavior—and the behavior becomes something people can take home.

Why BRUT cares

Enter hospitality without opening a space; earn repeated use, context, content, and potential retail.

Why FAUX cares

Create a proprietary guest experience with fashion/design relevance and a story that travels outside the room.

Why the customer cares

They get a useful, memorable ritual—not a logo placement—and an optional object connected to the experience.
